Mandarin Learning Reader
read it, hear it, say it, get graded

A free desktop app for learning to read and speak Mandarin

Read a Chinese book aloud and get every character graded.

Open any Chinese EPUB and the app splits it into short, speakable lines with tone-marked pinyin over every character. It reads each line in a neural Mandarin voice, records you saying it back, transcribes you with Whisper on your own machine, and marks each character right or missed. At 90% accuracy it moves to the next line on its own.

The gap

Reading practice that checks what you said

I'm a Cantonese speaker from Hong Kong learning Mandarin, and I read Traditional characters more easily than Simplified. Most Chinese reading tools show the text and at best read it to you; checking whether what you said matched the characters on the page is left to a tutor, who costs money and keeps a schedule. This app does the mechanical part of that job (did you say the characters on screen?) for free, whenever you happen to be reading.

The loop

Read, listen, speak, get graded

Read
One unit at a time with tone-marked pinyin above each character. Short sentences are joined until a unit has at least 7 characters (up to 15); long sentences are never cut
Listen
Six edge-tts neural voices, four Mainland and two Taiwan, at 0.5×, 0.75×, 1×, 1.25× or 1.5×. Each line's audio is cached, so replays are instant
Speak
Press Record, read the line, press again. faster-whisper (the "small" model by default) transcribes the recording on your machine
Graded
Each character is marked right or missed, with an accuracy score. 90% or higher advances to the next line; this can be switched off
Script
OpenCC converts the whole book between Simplified and Traditional from a menu

The grader

Alignment, so one slip doesn't cascade

The grader aligns the transcript against the original with difflib.SequenceMatcher, so a skipped character doesn't shift every mark after it. Whisper often writes Mandarin in Traditional characters whatever the book uses, so both sides are converted to Simplified before comparing (学 and 學 count as the same), while the display keeps the book's own characters.

Lenient mode adds a sound check. Where the alignment finds a substitution, it compares the pinyin of the two characters, ignoring tone by default, and a match counts toward the score (amber in the app). Mandarin has many homophones, and a learner who said the right syllable has done what the exercise asks even when Whisper picks a different character.

Day to day

It remembers where you were

Reopen the app and it returns to the same book, chapter and line, with your voice, speed and settings. Chapters are listed down the side, and pages that look like a cover, copyright notice or contents are flagged so you can skip them.

There are two interfaces over the same engine: a media-player layout with a karaoke-style stage, and a classic reader. Both have light and dark themes and keyboard shortcuts for every control. A command-line tool exposes the engine directly for listing chapters, previewing units and pinyin, and testing the grader.

Limitations

It grades characters and leaves tones unchecked

The score compares what Whisper heard with the characters on screen, which makes it a proxy for pronunciation; it does not assess tones. Whisper can be more forgiving than a native listener, and it can also mishear a correct reading. Lenient mode ignores tones by default when it compares pinyin.

Audio comes from Microsoft's online edge-tts service, so each new line needs a connection the first time it is played. Speech recognition runs offline after a one-time model download. The app ships no books: you supply your own EPUBs.

Built with

Python on your own machine, no cost

EPUB
ebooklib and BeautifulSoup for the spine, table of contents and text
Pinyin
pypinyin, phrase-aware for characters with several readings
Speech
edge-tts neural voices (online); faster-whisper speech-to-text (offline), with openai-whisper as a fallback
Grading
difflib alignment, OpenCC script normalization, optional pinyin match
Interface
Tkinter, two layouts; sounddevice for the microphone, pygame for playback
Sibling
